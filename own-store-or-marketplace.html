Own Store or Marketplace? A Decision Guide for Small Sellers in India

2 min read · Educational only

A marketplace gives you buyers but takes fees and sets rules. Your own store gives you control but you must find every customer yourself.

MarketplaceOwn store
TrafficBuilt inYou pay for it (ads) or build it slowly
FeesCommission and other charges, varies by categoryPayment fee and store subscription
ControlPlatform sets rules and pricesYou decide
Repeat customersPlatform owns the customerYou own the contact list
Best forFirst sales, testing productsBrands with repeat buyers

Example (illustrative numbers)

Product sells at ₹500. Marketplace fees of 15% = ₹75, so you keep ₹425 before product cost. Own store: payment fee ₹10 plus ₹150 ad cost per customer = ₹160, so you keep ₹340 on the first order.

The own store only wins when the same customer buys again.

Simple rule

Start on a marketplace to learn what sells. Open your own store once you have a product with repeat buyers.
