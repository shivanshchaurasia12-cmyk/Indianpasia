8 Warning Signs of a Fake Trading Signal Group

2 min read · Educational only

Many signal groups earn money from you, not from trading. Check for these signs:

Warning signWhat to check
Guaranteed or daily profitNo honest trader can promise this. Leave.
Only profit screenshotsScreenshots are easy to fake. Ask for a verified track record.
Pushes a "VIP" paid groupWhy would a winner sell signals instead of trading?
Tells you to use a specific app or linkFake apps show fake profits and block withdrawals.
No SEBI registrationSearch the name on SEBI's official list.
Pays to a personal UPI or accountReal firms take money in a company account.
Pressure and countdownsReal opportunities do not vanish in 10 minutes.
Strangers add you to the groupRandom invites are a common scam entry.

If you already sent money, stop paying more, save all chats and payment proofs, and report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
