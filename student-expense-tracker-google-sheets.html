Build a Student Expense Tracker in Google Sheets in 20 Minutes

2 min read · Educational only

You need a free Google account. Follow the steps in order.

  1. Open sheets.google.com and click Blank spreadsheet.
  2. Rename the tab to Expenses.
  3. In row 1 type: Date, Category, Note, Amount in columns A to D.
  4. Click B2:B500, then Data → Data validation → Add rule → Dropdown.
  5. Type your categories: Food, Travel, Books, Fun, Other. Click Done.
  6. Click the + at the bottom to add a new tab. Name it Summary.
  7. In Summary, type the categories in A2 to A6.
  8. In B2 type =SUMIF(Expenses!B:B, A2, Expenses!D:D) and drag it down to B6.
  9. In A8 type Total, and in B8 type =SUM(B2:B6).

Now add one row per spend. The Summary tab updates by itself.
