How to Read a Candlestick Chart: A Beginner Guide for TradingView

2 min read · Educational only

Each candle shows what price did in one time period (1 minute, 1 day, and so on).

PartMeaning
BodyDistance between open and close
Wicks (shadows)Highest and lowest price in that period
Green candleClosed higher than it opened (on most charts)
Red candleClosed lower than it opened

Timeframes

The same stock can look rising on a daily chart and falling on a 5-minute chart. Longer timeframes show the bigger trend and have less noise.

Practice (free, no money)

  1. Open TradingView, search any NSE stock, set the timeframe to 1D. Find the highest candle of the last month.
  2. Switch to 1W. Note if the trend looks different.
  3. Pick 5 candles and say for each: opened where, closed where, green or red.

This is for learning only. Candles do not predict the future.
