Revenge Trading: How One Loss Turns Into Four

2 min read · Educational only

Revenge trading means trying to win back a loss quickly, usually by trading bigger. It feels logical and it is how small losses become large ones.

Worked example

Account: ₹20,000. Plan: risk 2% (₹400) per trade.

TradeRevenge (doubling)LossAccount left
1₹400 risk-₹400₹19,600
2₹800 risk-₹800₹18,800
3₹1,600 risk-₹1,600₹17,200
4₹3,200 risk-₹3,200₹14,000

Four losses doubled each time cost ₹6,000 (30%). Four losses with the plan (2% each) cost about ₹1,552 (7.8%).

How to stop

  • Write your risk per trade before the day starts.
  • After 2 losses in a row, close the screen for the day.
  • Never increase size to "make it back".
