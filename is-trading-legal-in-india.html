Is Trading Legal in India? A Checklist to Run Before You Deposit Money

2 min read · Educational only

Buying shares on a recognised exchange through a registered broker is legal. But "trading" is also used to sell products that are not. Run these six checks first.

  1. Name the product. Shares, futures, options and mutual funds are different from forex, binary options or crypto "signals".
  2. Check the broker. Search the firm on the SEBI website's list of registered intermediaries.
  3. Check the exchange. Your trades should happen on a recognised exchange such as NSE or BSE.
  4. Check forex rules. RBI warns against unauthorised forex platforms and publishes an alert list. Do not deposit with unlisted ones.
  5. Check the payment. Money should go to the broker's official account, never to a personal UPI ID or wallet.
  6. Check the promise. Anyone guaranteeing returns or "daily profit" is a red flag.

If even one answer is unclear, do not deposit. Verify on the official regulator websites, not on the platform's own claims.
