Dropshipping Profit Maths: Where the Money in a ₹699 Order Actually Goes

2 min read · Educational only

A ₹699 sale does not mean ₹699 profit. Here is one order, line by line (figures are examples):

ItemAmount
Selling price₹699
Product cost from supplier₹300
Shipping₹70
Payment fee (about 2%)₹14
Left before ads₹315

Break-even ad cost

You can spend at most ₹315 on ads to get one order. Spend ₹400 and you lose ₹85 on every sale.

Break-even return on ad spend = 699 ÷ 315 = about 2.2. Every ₹1 of ads must bring ₹2.20 of sales just to break even.

Also count

  • Returns and failed deliveries (you often lose shipping both ways).
  • Your time and any tools or subscriptions.
  • Test with a small budget first.
